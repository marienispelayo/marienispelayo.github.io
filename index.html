Meet Your Pilot

On duty

I started in marketing at Florida State University, where I graduated cum laude with a minor in entrepreneurship. Since then, my career has been in B2B SaaS: product marketing at JP Global Digital, product management at Groundworks, and most recently product marketing at Neon One, where I led the commercialization of the platform’s first integrated Donor-Advised Fund payment solution.

Having worked in both product marketing and product management, I focus on the space where they meet: turning customer insight and usage data into positioning, launches, and tools that help revenue teams compete. I’m now pursuing my MBA in Brand & Product Management at UT Austin’s McCombs School of Business.
