Meet Your Pilot

On duty

My career has been rooted in product marketing from the start. I began as a product marketing associate at a startup, where the role was broad and hands-on: building campaigns, growing audiences, and learning firsthand how to reach people. As I grew into a product marketing manager role there, my focus expanded from capturing attention to explaining why a product matters, developing positioning, shaping go-to-market strategies, and giving teams the insights and tools to bring those strategies to life.

From there, I stepped into product management, using user feedback and behavioral data to shape features and improve digital experiences. That role gave me a new lens when I returned to product marketing: a closer understanding of the decisions behind a product, not just the story we tell about it.

Today, I’m pursuing my MBA in Brand & Product Management at UT Austin’s McCombs School of Business, building on those experiences and exploring what comes next.
